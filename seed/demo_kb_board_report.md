# Quarterly Investment Committee Strategy Report - FY 2024

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the strategic macroeconomic outlook, asset allocation targets, and performance mandates for Acme Asset Management's core funds for the fiscal year 2024.

## Macroeconomic Outlook
The Investment Committee anticipates a transitional year in 2024. We project central banks will pivot from their restrictive monetary policies by Q3, leading to a steepening yield curve. Consequently, we are adopting a "Risk-On" approach for H2 2024, favoring duration in fixed income and growth sectors in global equities.

## Fund Performance Targets (Alpha Generation)
The Board of Directors has established the following strict performance mandates for our core fund offerings, measured against their respective market benchmarks:

1. **US Core Equity Fund (USEQ)**: Target outperformance (Alpha) of **+150 bps (1.50%)** net of fees against the S&P 500 Total Return Index. The portfolio manager must achieve this while maintaining a Beta between 0.95 and 1.05.
2. **Global Alpha Equity (GBAQ)**: Target outperformance of **+250 bps (2.50%)** against the MSCI World Index. This aggressive target justifies the higher 75 bps management fee.
3. **US Core Fixed Income (USFX)**: Target outperformance of **+50 bps (0.50%)** against the Bloomberg US Aggregate Bond Index. We expect this to be driven by active duration management rather than taking on excessive credit risk.
4. **Emerging Markets Opportunities (EMEQ)**: Target outperformance of **+300 bps (3.00%)** against the FTSE Emerging Markets Index, driven primarily by our overweight position in Southeast Asian technology equities.

## Client Acquisition and AUM Goals
For 2024, the strategic directive is to aggressively scale Total Assets Under Management (AUM) to cross the **$3.5 Billion** mark globally. 

Our client acquisition strategy will shift focus:
- **Institutional Clients**: We expect $200M in net new flows from endowments and pensions. Marketing cost of acquisition for institutional AUM should not exceed 10 bps (0.10%) of the acquired assets.
- **Retail Clients**: We are launching massive digital ad campaigns to capture retail ETF flows. The target is $50M in net new retail flows.

## Risk Management & Compliance
Any fund that underperforms its benchmark for three consecutive quarters will trigger a mandatory portfolio manager review and an automatic 20% reduction in risk limits (Value at Risk). Liquidity stress testing must be performed monthly for all High Net Worth portfolios.
