# ESG Fund Scoring Report — FY2020 to FY2023

## Overview

This report covers quarterly ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) scores for all four funds managed by the firm. Scores are assessed by our ESG research team on a 100-point scale and mapped to letter ratings: AAA (≥80), AA (≥70), A (≥60), BBB (≥50), BB (≥40), B (below 40).

**Scoring period:** Q1 2020 through Q4 2023 (16 quarters per fund, 64 total assessments)

## Methodology Notes

- **ESG Total Score** = weighted average: Environment (35%), Social (30%), Governance (35%)
- **Carbon Intensity**: tonnes CO₂ equivalent per $1M revenue (scope 1+2)
- **Water Usage Intensity**: cubic metres per $1M revenue (not tracked pre-2022)
- **ESG Rating Scale**: AAA (≥80) | AA (70–79) | A (60–69) | BBB (50–59) | BB (40–49) | B (<40)
- Assessments performed by ESG Research & Analytics team using MSCI ESG Research data as primary source, supplemented by proprietary scoring for data gaps
