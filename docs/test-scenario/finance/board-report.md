## Executive Summary

For fiscal year 2024 the Board set a **total revenue target of $9.8M** across all
regions and product categories. Management was directed to prioritize the
Electronics and Software categories, which carry the highest margins.

## Targets

- **Annual revenue target:** $9,800,000
- **Q4 revenue target:** $3,100,000 (seasonal uplift expected in Nov–Dec)
- **Gross margin target:** 38%
- **Budget variance tolerance:** within ±5% of plan

## Regional Guidance

- **West** is the priority growth region; the Board approved additional sales
  headcount and a target of **$2.9M** for the region.
- **North** is the most mature region; hold spend flat and defend a **$2.3M** target.
- **East** and **South** are expected to deliver **$2.3M** and **$2.3M** respectively.

## Product Strategy

- **Electronics:** flagship category — protect pricing, target 45% of revenue.
- **Software:** fastest growth — invest in the subscription motion.
- **Furniture:** steady; manage inventory carefully.
- **Office Supplies:** low margin; bundle with other categories.

## Risks

- Supply constraints in Electronics could cap Q4 upside.
- Currency headwinds in the East region.

## Recommendation

The Board recommends a monthly review of **actual revenue vs. budget** by region
and category, with escalation when variance exceeds the ±5% tolerance.
